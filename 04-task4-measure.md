# Task 4 – Measure


Completion shows reach only. The metrics therefore cover reach, behaviour and outcomes and are compared with the Month 1 baseline (ICDFA, n.d., slide 8; NIST, 2024).


*Table 7. Metrics: calculation, source, owner, frequency and meaning of a change.*


| **Metric (type)** | **Calculation** | **Data source** | **Owner and review** | **What a change means** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **M1** Role-based completion (reach) | Completed ÷ assigned × 100, by audience; warehouse = huddle sign-sheet entries ÷ staff on shift roster | LMS; huddle sheets; HR roster | Awareness Coordinator; monthly | Low warehouse figure signals access or timing problems, not unwillingness. High alone proves reach only. |
| **M2** Simulation report rate (behaviour) | Staff who reported the simulated email ÷ delivered × 100 | Simulation platform; mailbox | Awareness Coordinator; quarterly | Rise = growing reporting habit (O3). Fall with steady clicks = friction in the route. |
| **M3** Click rate and repeat clickers (behaviour) | Clicked or entered data ÷ delivered × 100; repeat = failed 2 of last 4 | Simulation platform | Awareness Coordinator; quarterly | Fall = fewer risky actions. Unchanged repeaters = coaching or role mismatch. Compare same difficulty only. |
| **M4** Payment-verification rate (behaviour) | Sampled bank-detail changes with documented call-back ÷ sampled × 100 | Accounts-payable tickets | Finance Controller; monthly | Below 95% means the routine is not followed: a leadership decision on making it a system-enforced step (O1). |
| **M5** Breached or reused passwords (behaviour) | Flagged accounts ÷ total accounts × 100 | Identity-system screening | IT Operations Manager; quarterly | Fall = reuse reduced (O2). A jump after a public breach is expected: repeat the campaign. |
| **M6** Human-caused incidents and time to report (outcome) | Confirmed incidents with human cause per quarter; median minutes from event to report; fraud loss | Incident log; finance records | Head of Information Security; quarterly | Counts may rise first as reporting improves. Success is lower impact and loss, and shorter time to report. |


**Reporting to leadership [Proposal].** Monthly: a one-line red-amber-green note from the Awareness Coordinator to the Head of Information Security. Quarterly: a one-page dashboard to the sponsor and executives in risk terms, one line per risk (R1–R3) showing result against baseline and target, what it means, and the action. **Limitations stated every time:** small groups (finance has 25 people, so counts are shown beside percentages); results vary with lure difficulty; reports and incidents rise before they fall; there is no control group; and email filtering, not training, also affects incidents. **Decisions requested** are specific, for example: make call-back a system-enforced step; approve night-shift learning time; fund a new-scam module from the reserve.
