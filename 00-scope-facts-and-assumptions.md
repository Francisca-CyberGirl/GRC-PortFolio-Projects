# Scope, facts and assumptions


The program follows the five-stage lifecycle: Assess, Plan, Deliver, Measure, Improve (ICDFA, n.d., slide 3). Labels: **[Fact]** is stated in the brief; **[Assumption]** is my choice about systems, staffing or resources; **[Proposal]** is my design; **[Target]** is a planning choice, not a Northbridge result. Page guide: report pages 1–5, calendar page 6, reflection, declaration and references page 7.
