# Task 5 – Improve and sustain


**Quarterly review [Proposal].** In Months 3, 6, 9 and 12, in the fourth week after data are cut. Sixty minutes. Chair: Head of Information Security. Attendees: Awareness Coordinator (secretary), Finance Controller, IT Operations Manager, HR People Partner, Warehouse Operations Manager with a night-shift representative, Sales Director or delegate, and the Data Protection Officer; the sponsor receives the summary and joins in Month 12. Inputs: the dashboard against baseline, incident and near-miss log, simulation results by audience, learner feedback and the change calendar. Outputs: each decision and action is recorded in a shared tracker with owner, due date, linked risk and status; the tracker is reviewed first at the next meeting and overdue items go to the sponsor (ICDFA, n.d., slide 9).


**Triggers for content updates [Proposal].** Content is revised within 15 working days (urgent warnings within two) after: (1) a human-caused incident or near miss; (2) an emerging scam seen by staff, finance or threat alerts; (3) a process or system change, such as a new bank portal or handheld system; (4) a metric breach: verification below 90% in any month, click rate up five points quarter on quarter, or report rate falling two quarters running; (5) an audit finding or regulatory change.


**Listening to learners [Proposal].** A two-question pulse (useful? clear?) after each module or huddle, by QR code or kiosk buttons; four 30-minute focus groups a year (one day-shift and one night-shift group in Months 4 and 10, rotating sites, in paid time); a session with finance after the tabletop; two sales calls. One volunteer champion per shift per site gives fortnightly feedback. Response rates are tracked by audience and shift to spot who is not being heard, and a “You said, we did” note follows each review, covering changes to timing, language, format and access.


**Recognising good behaviour [Proposal].** (1) “Good Catch”: every genuine report gets a personal thank-you within one working day, and a monthly shout-out in huddles and the newsletter with the person’s consent, including fast reporting of mistakes. (2) “Safe Team” award: each quarter one team (a warehouse shift, finance sub-team or sales region) wins a USD 500 team voucher (4 × 500 = 2,000) for the biggest improvement on a behaviour measure, not the highest score. Nobody is named for making a mistake.
