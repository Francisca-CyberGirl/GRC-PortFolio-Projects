# ISO 27001 Stage 2 Audit Assessment Report

## Executive Summary

A Stage 2 audit assessment was conducted to evaluate the effectiveness of the organization's Information Security Management System (ISMS).

The assessment reviewed governance processes, personnel security controls, physical security measures, vulnerability management activities, and implementation of risk treatment actions.

While several controls were operating effectively, significant weaknesses were identified in governance oversight, personnel security documentation, and implementation of approved risk treatment measures.

One major nonconformity was identified relating to physical access protection for critical infrastructure.

Based on the evidence reviewed, certification readiness could not be confirmed at the time of assessment.

---

## Assessment Scope

The assessment covered:

- Personnel Security
- Information Security Governance
- Physical Security Controls
- Risk Treatment Implementation
- Vulnerability Management
- Corrective Action Processes

Evidence was collected through:

- Documentation Review
- Personnel Interviews
- Record Sampling
- Site Inspection
- Remediation Verification

---

## Assessment Findings

### Finding 1 – Personnel Screening Records

**Classification:** Nonconformity

Human Resources personnel confirmed that background screening is conducted for new employees.

However, a sample review of employee records identified multiple personnel files without documented evidence that screening activities had been completed.

Although the process appears to exist, implementation could not be consistently verified through available records.

#### Risk Impact

- Reduced assurance over personnel security controls.
- Incomplete audit evidence.
- Potential compliance concerns.

---

### Finding 2 – Information Security Policy Review

**Classification:** Nonconformity

The Information Security Policy had not undergone formal review for an extended period.

No evidence was available to demonstrate periodic management review or approval.

Policies must remain current and aligned with organizational objectives, regulatory obligations, and evolving risk conditions.

#### Risk Impact

- Outdated governance documentation.
- Reduced management oversight.
- Potential misalignment with current risk environment.

---

### Finding 3 – Server Room Physical Access Controls

**Classification:** Major Nonconformity

The organization's risk register identified unauthorized physical access to the server room as a high-risk scenario.

The approved risk treatment plan specified implementation of biometric access controls.

During physical inspection, only a traditional key lock was observed.

This demonstrated failure to implement an approved risk treatment measure for a critical asset.

#### Risk Impact

- Unauthorized physical access.
- Potential compromise of critical infrastructure.
- Failure of risk treatment implementation.

---

### Finding 4 – Vulnerability Management

**Classification:** Conformity

Scheduled penetration testing activities were conducted as planned.

Review of remediation records confirmed that critical vulnerabilities identified during the most recent assessment were resolved within established response timelines.

Evidence demonstrated an effective vulnerability management process supported by timely remediation activities.

#### Positive Observations

- Effective vulnerability identification.
- Timely remediation.
- Strong operational response capability.

---

## Audit Conclusion

The assessment identified several areas where controls were functioning effectively, particularly within vulnerability management and remediation activities.

However, weaknesses were identified within governance, personnel security, and risk treatment implementation processes.

The most significant issue involved failure to implement approved physical access controls for a high-risk server room despite inclusion within the organization's approved risk treatment plan.

Based on the evidence reviewed, certification readiness could not be confirmed at the time of assessment.

Corrective actions should be implemented and verified before certification activities proceed.

---

## Corrective Action Review

### Submitted Action

> We have ordered the biometric lock and it will be installed next week.

### Evaluation

#### Is the correction clearly defined?

Yes.

The organization identified a specific action to replace the existing key lock with a biometric access control solution.

#### Does it address the root cause?

No.

The response focuses only on implementing the missing control and does not explain why the approved risk treatment was not implemented originally.

#### Is it appropriate to the severity of the issue?

Yes.

The proposed biometric control aligns with the organization's approved risk treatment strategy.

#### Is there a realistic implementation timeline?

Partially.

A timeline was provided but supporting implementation evidence was not available.

#### Does it prevent recurrence?

No.

The action does not include process improvements, oversight mechanisms, or accountability measures that would prevent future implementation failures.

### Assessment Outcome

The proposed action may be accepted as an immediate correction but should not be accepted as a complete corrective action.

Additional evidence should include:

- Root cause analysis.
- Verification of implementation.
- Risk treatment tracking improvements.
- Ongoing monitoring activities.

---

## Recommendations

### Immediate Priorities

1. Implement biometric access controls for the server room.
2. Perform root cause analysis for failed risk treatment implementation.
3. Update and formally review the Information Security Policy.
4. Verify completeness of personnel screening records.

### Long-Term Improvements

1. Strengthen risk treatment tracking processes.
2. Establish periodic policy review schedules.
3. Improve governance oversight of corrective actions.
4. Introduce formal control verification procedures.

---

## Project Outcome

This assessment demonstrates the ability to evaluate ISMS effectiveness, identify control weaknesses, assess organizational risk exposure, review corrective actions, and develop professional audit conclusions aligned with ISO 27001 auditing principles.
