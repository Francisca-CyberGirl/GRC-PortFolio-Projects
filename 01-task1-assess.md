# Task 1 – Assess


## 1.1 Top three human-driven risks


*Table 1. Priority risks, justified from the scenario and external evidence.*


| **Risk** | **Scenario evidence [Fact] and reasoning** | **Most exposed** |
|---|---|---|
| **R1 Invoice and payment-redirection fraud** | Recent invoice-fraud attempts [Fact]. One accepted bank-detail change redirects a whole supplier payment. Reported business email compromise losses were USD 3.05 billion across 24,768 complaints in the FBI’s 2025 report (FBI IC3, 2026, via Adams and Reese, 2026). | Finance, leaders, office |
| **R2 Reused and weak passwords** | Two staff reused passwords after a breach [Fact]. Attackers replay stolen passwords on other services; credential abuse appeared in 39% of breaches in the 2026 DBIR (Verizon, 2026, via Abnormal Security, 2026). Shared kiosk logins may make this worse [Assumption, verify in baseline]. | All account holders, sales, IT admins |
| **R3 Mishandling regulated customer data and slow reporting** | The company handles regulated data and the security team is small [Fact], so it depends on staff to speak up early. Staff who fear reprisals report late or not at all (NCSC, n.d.). 62% of breaches involved the human element (Verizon, 2026, via Abnormal Security, 2026). | Warehouse, sales, office |


Considered and not ranked: device loss, tailgating and ransomware. The scenario gives no evidence for them, so they appear as topics inside the three risks above, not as separate priorities.


## 1.2 Audiences and their needs


*Table 2. Six audiences (A1); new joiners are handled through onboarding (Task 3).*


| **Audience (size)** | **Work context and access** | **Distinct needs** |
|---|---|---|
| **Warehouse and operations (420)** | Shared kiosks and handhelds, two shifts, three sites, little desk time, mostly no email [A2] | Very short, practical, in-shift help; no reliance on email; simple language; an easy way to report from the floor |
| **General office (220)** | Email, shared drives, customer records | Spotting fakes; safe sharing; password habits that are easy to keep |
| **Finance and procurement (25)** | Supplier records, bank-detail changes, payment approvals | A firm verification routine and practice with realistic fraud pressure |
| **Remote sales (100)** | Laptops, phones, CRM with customer data, public Wi-Fi | Flexible online learning; device and data rules away from the office; spotting spoofed customers |
| **Executives and senior managers (20)** | Approve payments; often impersonated | Short briefings in risk terms; how their requests can be abused; role-modelling |
| **IT administrators and security team (15)** | Privileged accounts, identity and email tools | Hands-on skills; handling reports well; enforcing controls that support the behaviour |


## 1.3 Pre-launch baseline


The baseline is captured in Month 1, before any training reaches staff, so later results have a fair comparison (ICDFA, n.d., slide 4). No baseline figures are invented here.


*Table 3. Baseline measures, evidence and owners.*


| **Baseline measure** | **Evidence source** | **Owner** |
|---|---|---|
| B1 Click and report rates in one benign simulation to all 440 email users (A2) | Simulation platform and the reporting mailbox | Awareness Coordinator |
| B2 Share of the last 30 bank-detail changes with proof of call-back to a number already on file | Accounts-payable tickets and records | Finance Controller |
| B3 Accounts with breached or reused passwords; share of accounts with MFA | Identity system reports (A6) | IT Operations Manager |
| B4 Reports received, median time from event to report and human-caused incidents in the last 12 months | Incident log and security mailbox | Head of Information Security |
| B5 Ten-question anonymous survey on policy awareness and “I would report a mistake without worry” (paper and QR code for warehouse) | Survey results; target response of at least 60% in each audience | Awareness Coordinator |
| B6 Practice check at three sites: shared logins, screen locks, challenging unknown visitors | Supervisor observation checklist | Warehouse Operations Manager |
