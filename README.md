## Summary of the design

- **Assess:** three risks (invoice fraud, reused passwords, regulated-data mishandling and slow reporting), six audiences (800 staff) and a six-part Month 1 baseline.
- **Plan:** three measurable objectives (O1 call-back verification at least 95% by Month 6; O2 flagged passwords down 50% by Month 9; O3 simulated-phish report rate at least 60% by Month 12), a named sponsor and owners, USD 16,000 and 896 of 960 team hours.
- **Deliver:** formats per audience, including supervisor-led huddles for warehouse staff, onboarding, supportive phishing simulations and a no-blame reporting route.
- **Measure:** six metrics covering reach, behaviour and outcomes, each with calculation, source, owner, frequency and meaning, plus quarterly leadership reporting.
- **Improve:** quarterly reviews, update triggers, shift-based feedback and two recognition schemes; a traceability table ties each risk to its audience, objective, activity, metric and review decision.
