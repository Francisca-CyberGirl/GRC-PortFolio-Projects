# Executive Briefing for SecureMobile Risk Assessment

## Situation Summary

The SecureMobile banking platform was assessed to identify critical cybersecurity risks affecting transaction integrity, customer data, and session security.

The focus of this lab was to quantify these risks in financial terms and support security decisions with measurable business impact.

---

## Top 3 Risks (Financial Impact)

- **Database Injection** → Highest exposure (~$31.25M ALE)  
- **Session Hijacking** → High probability account compromise (~$3M ALE)  
- **API Authentication Bypass** → Critical transaction risk (~$750K ALE)  

These risks represent the highest threat to customer trust, platform stability, and financial performance.

---

## Control Strategy and Cost

Recommended controls:

- **API Security Gateway** → $350,000 (90% effectiveness)  
- **Web Application Firewall (WAF)** → $150,000 (75% effectiveness)  
- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** → $200,000 (95% effectiveness)  

The total investment is justified by the level of risk reduction across all major attack paths.

---

## Risk Reduction Outcome

Applying these controls results in:

- Major reduction in database exposure through WAF  
- Strong reduction in API abuse through API Gateway  
- Significant reduction in account takeover through MFA  

The overall risk profile shifts from high exposure to a more controlled state.

---

## ROI Analysis

- **MFA** delivers the highest ROI due to strong protection and lower cost  
- **WAF** provides the best cost-to-protection balance  
- **API Gateway** strengthens core transaction security and long-term resilience  

These controls provide strong financial value through reduced exposure and lower incident cost.
