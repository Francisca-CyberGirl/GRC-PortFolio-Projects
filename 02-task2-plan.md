# Task 2 – Plan


## 2.1 Measurable objectives


*Table 4. Objectives. All targets are [Target]: planning choices, compared against Table 3.*


| **Objective** | **Risk** | **Evidence** | **Owner** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **O1** By the end of Month 6, at least 95% of sampled supplier bank-detail changes show call-back verification to a known number, and no fraudulent payment is released in Months 1–12. | R1 | Monthly sample of all changes (about 40, A7); finance loss records | Finance Controller |
| **O2** By the end of Month 9, accounts flagged as breached or reused fall by at least 50% from the Month 1 baseline. | R2 | Quarterly identity-system screening | IT Operations Manager |
| **O3** By the end of Month 12, at least 60% of staff who receive a simulated phish report it (at least 30% by Month 6), at least 85% of all reports get a human reply within one working day, and median time to report is under 60 minutes for email events. | R3 | Simulation platform; report queue time stamps | Head of Information Security |


## 2.2 Sponsor and owners


| **Role** | **Responsibilities in this program** |
|---|---|
| **Executive sponsor: Chief Operating Officer** | Signs the no-blame statement; approves budget and paid learning time (about 1,545 staff hours a year); receives the quarterly report; decides escalated actions. The COO runs the warehouses, so can release shift time (ICDFA, n.d., slide 5). |
| **Program owner: Head of Information Security** | Owns the design and the link from risk to activity; chairs quarterly reviews; owns O3 and leadership reporting. |
| **Awareness Coordinator (0.5 FTE security analyst)** | Builds and schedules content; runs simulations and coaching; collects metrics; keeps the action log. |
| **Finance Controller** | Owns O1 and the payment-verification procedure; releases accounts-payable staff for sessions. |
| **IT Operations Manager** | Owns O2; supplies identity data; builds and runs the reporting button, form and mailbox. |
| **HR People Partner** | Runs onboarding records and the recognition scheme; keeps learning records. |
| **Warehouse Operations Manager; Sales Director** | Release staff; supervisors lead huddles; site champions give feedback; Sales Director secures sales attendance. |
| **Data Protection Officer** | Checks that content on regulated data is accurate; decides on any breach notification. |


**Resources [Assumption].** Cash (A4), USD 16,000: simulation and reporting licences 440 × 6 = 2,640; off-the-shelf microlearning 800 × 5 = 4,000; print and signage 2,400; recognition 2,000; two external workshops 3,000; reserve for new scams 1,960. Team time (A3, 960 hours): content 240, simulations 100, coaching and report triage 120, huddle cards 88, measurement and reporting 160, quarterly reviews 48, feedback 60, recognition 40, onboarding upkeep 40 = 896 hours, leaving 64 spare.


## 2.3 Topics by audience


*Table 5. Topics linked to the risks in Table 1.*


| **Audience** | **Topics** | **Risks** |
|---|---|---|
| **Warehouse and operations** | Own login, never shared; lock the screen; fake driver, supplier or delivery requests; handling manifests with customer details; how to report from the floor | R2, R3, R1 |
| **General office** | Spotting phishing and fake invoices; passphrases, MFA and no reuse; sharing customer data safely; reporting | R1, R2, R3 |
| **Finance and procurement** | Bank-detail change routine with call-back; urgency and executive-impersonation tricks; invoice red flags; escalating doubts | R1 |
| **Remote sales** | Public Wi-Fi and device safety; customer data stays in the CRM; spoofed customer requests; reporting a lost device | R2, R3, R1 |
| **Executives and senior managers** | How executive names are abused; never approve payments by chat or phone alone; their own reporting; reading the dashboard | R1, R3 |
| **IT administrators and security team** | Separate admin accounts; password screening and MFA enforcement; triaging reports; supportive replies | R2, R3 |
